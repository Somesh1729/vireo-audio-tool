## What actually happened

The roster shows the Indore Night shift was eliminated on 30 June
2025 (three agents left, two moved to the Day shift, no
replacements). Chat is still promised as 24x7 in the support policy,
with overnight coverage meant to come from that shift. It no longer
exists.

Every chat ticket that comes in overnight now sits untouched until
the Morning shift logs on at 06:00. Before the change, overnight
chat tickets breached 7.8% of the time. Since the change: **99.9%**.
Median first-response time went from 4 minutes to over 7 hours. Chat
tickets created during the day, in the same period, stayed flat —
this isn't the desk getting worse generally, it's this one window.
